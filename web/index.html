NOST4LGIA

Cable TV, but weirder — a retro guide of always-on channels.
▶  Open the Guide 📺  Put it on your TV

What is this?

Nost4lgia is a channel-surfing TV guide built out of public-domain and publicly-archived video. Every channel plays on a continuous schedule, like real cable — flip to a channel and you join whatever is "on" right now, mid-show. No account, no queue, no autoplay-next-episode. Just a dial and a guide.

▲▼ change channel  ·  ◀▶ browse the guide / seek  ·  Enter watch  ·  0–9 jump to a channel  ·  Esc back

How it works

A real schedule

Each channel loops its library on a fixed timeline anchored to the clock, so the grid shows what's on now and what's up next.

Join live

Tuning in seeks to the current moment of the show instead of restarting it — change the channel and you drop into the middle, just like TV.

Set reminders

Pick a show airing later and it'll ask if you want to switch over when it starts.

Where the video comes from

Streams come straight from the Internet Archive. This is a hobby project for personal, nostalgic viewing of archived and public-domain material.

Made by Nicola Riker Urdaneta. Inspired by Spelunkr — Nost4lgia is an independent, open-source take with its own channels, guide and TV-first interface. Source: github.com/nicolascribbles/nost4lgia.
